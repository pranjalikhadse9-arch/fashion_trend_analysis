# Fashion Trends Analysis

## About the Project

Fashion trends keep changing, and customers often choose products based on their style, price, brand, and available discounts. In this project, I worked with fashion retail data related to Reliance Trends to understand different product categories, brand preferences, pricing, and discount patterns.

Using Python, I explored the dataset to find which brands and categories have more products, how discounts vary across different products, and how fashion preferences differ among men, women, and kids. I also analyzed different price segments to understand how products are distributed across budget, mid-range, premium, and luxury categories.

## Project Objectives

The main objective of this project was to understand fashion retail data and identify useful patterns. I focused on analyzing popular brands and product categories, comparing discounts across products, exploring gender-based preferences, and studying the distribution of products across different price segments. The project also helped me understand how pricing and discount strategies can be analyzed using data.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains information about brands, product names, product IDs, original prices, discounted prices, discount amounts, discount percentages, gender, and product categories. It also includes price segments such as Budget, Mid, Premium, and Luxury, along with discount tiers such as No Discount, Light, Moderate, and Heavy. These details helped me compare products and explore different patterns in the fashion retail market.

## Tools and Technologies Used

I completed this project using Python in Jupyter Notebook. I used Pandas for data cleaning, data manipulation, grouping, and analysis. Matplotlib and Seaborn were used to create visualizations and make the results easier to understand.

## Work Performed

I started by checking missing values, reviewing the dataset, and preparing the columns for analysis. Then, I explored numerical and categorical data to understand product prices, discounts, brands, and categories.

I analyzed the most common brands and product categories and compared product types across different genders. I also studied discount tiers, average discount percentages, and the relationship between product prices and discounts using different charts.

Finally, I explored gender-based product preferences and compared the distribution of products across Budget, Mid, Premium, and Luxury price segments. I used bar charts, histograms, box plots, scatter plots, pivot tables, and heatmaps to present different aspects of the analysis.

## Key Learnings

While working on this project, I gained practical experience in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), Pandas operations, grouping, value counts, pivot tables, and data visualization. I also learned how to compare product categories, analyze discount patterns, identify outliers, and explore pricing trends using Python.

## Conclusion

This project helped me understand how data analysis can be used to explore fashion retail trends and customer preferences. By analyzing brands, categories, discounts, gender, and price segments, I gained a better understanding of how different products can be compared using data. Overall, this project improved my Python skills and gave me practical experience in working with a real-world style of retail dataset.
